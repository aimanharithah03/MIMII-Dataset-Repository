One deliberate choice: I pinned tensorflow<2.16. That's not arbitrary — TensorFlow 2.16+ switched to Keras 3, which changes how .h5 files get serialized, and DEEPCRAFT Model Converter (Section 11 of the documentation) expects the pre-Keras-3 .h5 convention. Pinning it avoids hitting that same issue again, unless you'd rather stay on the newer TensorFlow and save as .keras instead when the time comes
